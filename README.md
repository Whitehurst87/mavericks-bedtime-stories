# Maverick's Bedtime Stories

A storybook game: Maverick's bedroom is the hub, the shelf holds his bedtime
stories as books, and each book is a small playable episode.

Play it at https://whitehurst87.github.io/mavericks-bedtime-stories/

Controls: WASD or arrows to walk, E to talk and grab, M for sound. In the
storybook: arrows pick a book, E reads it, F opens everything, B goes back to
the room.

Progress and medals are saved in the browser, per device.
